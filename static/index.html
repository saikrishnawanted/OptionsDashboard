TBS strategy rules

Sell one ATM CE and one ATM PE at 09:18, then 09:45, 10:15, 10:45, 11:15, 11:45, 12:15 and 12:45 IST. Select the expiry before starting.

  1. First straddle: 20% stop-loss per leg. Later straddles: 30% per leg.
  2. Buy stop-limit: trigger = entry × (1 + SL%). Limit = trigger × 1.02. Round both upward to the broker tick.
  3. One lot per leg: NIFTY 65, SENSEX 20. Broker metadata must match.
  4. No re-entry after a stop. Close remaining legs at 15:14. No daily loss cutoff.
  5. The heatmap tracks 10–100% counterfactual stops from observed ticks. Best SL is hindsight only and never drives orders.

Stop-limit orders can remain unfilled after gaps. Keep the terminal running and connected for scheduled exits; broker stops remain at the broker when the app is offline.
